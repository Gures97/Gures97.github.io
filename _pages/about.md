I have five years of experience in the embedded systems field. In my work, I focused on developing tools and scripts to support the production and testing of devices - both on the hardware and software level.

I was the originator and main developer of an internal scripting language and environment for automated testing of the company's hardware. My solutions significantly improved the testing process and were successfully adopted within the organization.

I have a strong sense of practical needs within technical teams - I can identify recurring problems and deliver concrete, effective solutions.

## 🔍 What I Do Best

* Designing internal tools and scripting environments that directly solve workflow issues
* Bridging gaps between software and hardware in embedded systems
* Spotting inefficiencies in processes and delivering practical, working solutions
* Leading and coordinating small technical teams in development projects
* Writing maintainable, purpose-driven code with a focus on simplicity and clarity


## 🛠️ Key Projects

### Test Automation Framework

Designed and implemented a custom scripting language and execution environment for automated testing of embedded devices. Used daily by production teams, this tool streamlined the validation process and reduced manual effort.

### Internal Tools for Production

Developed a suite of support scripts and utilities for configuring devices, analyzing logs, and improving production workflows.

### Embedded Team Leadership

Led a team of embedded developers in a complex industrial automation project. Coordinated cross-functional tasks and contributed directly to both hardware-related and software aspects.

## ⚙️ Tech Stack

**Languages:** C, Python, Bash\
**Tools:** Git, Docker, GitLab CI/CD, Make\
**Systems:** Linux, RTOS, Raspberry Pi\
**Other:** 3D printing workflow, electronics prototyping

## 💡 How I Work

I believe good engineering is about solving the right problems with simple and durable solutions. I prioritize readability and maintainability in code, clear communication in teams, and a hands-on approach to technical challenges. I enjoy diving deep into low-level systems while keeping a practical mindset.
